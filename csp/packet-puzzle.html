Packet Puzzle

CSP Unit 2 · Lesson 5: Packets

Big messages don't travel across the Internet in one piece. They're chopped into small packets, and each packet finds its own way through the routers. In this activity, each packet holds just 8 characters. That's the same limit as Code.org's Internet Simulator. (Real packets hold more, but the problems are the same.) Finish all 3 levels, then answer the questions at the bottom and turn them in on Teams.

Words to know
Datastream
The flow of data from one device to another, sent as a series of packets.
Packet
A small chunk of data sent over a network. A big message is split into many packets.
Packet metadata
Extra information in a packet that isn't part of the message itself, like where it's going or which number packet it is.
Protocol
An agreed-upon set of rules for how computers send and receive data.
UDP (User Datagram Protocol)
A protocol that sends packets and doesn't check whether they all arrived. Fast, but things can go wrong.
TCP (Transmission Control Protocol)
A protocol that numbers packets, puts them back in order, and asks for missing ones to be sent again. Reliable, but slower.
