You'll play four weeks. Each week, the rules for sending messages change. Finish all four, then answer the questions at the bottom and turn them in on Teams.

Words to know
Broadcast
Sending one message to everyone on a network at the same time.
Protocol
An agreed-upon set of rules for how computers send and receive messages.
IP address
A unique number given to every device on the Internet. Computers store it in binary.
Internet Protocol (IP)
The shared rules every Internet device follows for addressing messages, including where the sender's and receiver's IP addresses go.
